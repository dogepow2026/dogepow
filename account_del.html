Account & Data Deletion Request

Delete Account

Users can request permanent deletion of their DogePow account and associated data.

Deleted Data Includes

  • Profile information
  • Wallet address
  • Rewards history
  • Mining progress

Deletion Time

All account data will be permanently deleted within 30 days after the request is submitted.

How To Request Deletion

  1. Open the DogePow app
  2. Go to Profile → Delete Account
  3. Confirm your deletion request
