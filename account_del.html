Delete Account

Users can request deletion of their DogePow account and associated data directly from the app.

Data Scheduled for Deletion

  • Profile information
  • Wallet address
  • Rewards history
  • Mining progress
  • Subscription-related app data

Important Information

  • After submitting a deletion request, the account will be scheduled for permanent deletion after 30 days.
  • If you log back into your account within the 30-day period, the deletion request will be automatically canceled and your account will remain active.
  • Once the 30-day period is completed, all associated data will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

How to Request Deletion

  • 1. Open the DogePow app
  • 2. Go to Profile > Delete Account
  • 3. Confirm deletion request
